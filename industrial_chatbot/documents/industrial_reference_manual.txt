INDUSTRIAL SYSTEMS TECHNICAL REFERENCE MANUAL
Version 3.2 | General Purpose Industrial Guide
==============================================

SECTION 1: BOILER SYSTEMS
--------------------------
1.1 Overview
A boiler is a closed vessel in which fluid (generally water) is heated. The heated or vaporized fluid exits the boiler for use in various processes including heating, power generation, and sterilization.

1.2 Types of Boilers
- Fire-tube boilers: Hot gases pass through tubes surrounded by water. Used for low to medium pressure applications (up to 20 bar).
- Water-tube boilers: Water passes through tubes surrounded by hot gases. Used for high pressure applications (up to 160 bar) and large capacities.
- Electric boilers: Use electric resistance or immersion heating elements. Suitable for facilities with no gas supply.

1.3 Key Parameters
- Maximum Operating Pressure (MOP): 15 bar (fire-tube), 100 bar (water-tube)
- Normal Operating Temperature: 100°C – 180°C for hot water; 120°C – 250°C for steam
- Safety valve set pressure: Must not exceed 110% of MOP
- Efficiency range: 80% – 95% for modern condensing boilers

1.4 Safety Guidelines
- Never operate above maximum rated pressure
- Inspect water level gauge daily
- Blow down the boiler weekly to remove sludge
- Test safety valves monthly
- Annual inspection by certified engineer is mandatory

SECTION 2: HYDRAULIC SYSTEMS
-----------------------------
2.1 Overview
Hydraulic systems use pressurized fluid to transmit power. They are used in heavy machinery, manufacturing presses, aircraft controls, and excavators.

2.2 Components
- Hydraulic Pump: Converts mechanical energy to hydraulic energy. Types: gear, vane, piston.
- Hydraulic Cylinder: Converts hydraulic energy back to linear mechanical force.
- Control Valves: Directional (to route flow), pressure (to limit pressure), flow (to regulate speed).
- Reservoir: Stores hydraulic fluid, typically 2–3x the pump flow rate capacity.
- Filters: Keep fluid clean. Replace when differential pressure exceeds 3.5 bar.

2.3 Operating Specifications
- System pressure range: 70 – 350 bar depending on application
- Hydraulic fluid temperature: Max 60°C continuous, 80°C peak
- Recommended fluid: ISO 46 mineral oil for most industrial applications
- Flow rate: Measured in liters per minute (LPM)

2.4 Maintenance Schedule
- Daily: Check fluid level and look for leaks
- Weekly: Check pump noise and system pressure
- Monthly: Test safety relief valves; inspect hose fittings
- 6 months: Change hydraulic fluid and filters
- Annual: Full system inspection and pressure testing

SECTION 3: ELECTRICAL SYSTEMS
-------------------------------
3.1 Motor Specifications
Industrial AC motors are classified by their frame size (NEMA or IEC standards).
- Single-phase motors: Up to 3 kW, used for small machines
- Three-phase motors: 0.5 kW to several MW, most common in industry
- Efficiency classes: IE1 (standard), IE2 (high), IE3 (premium), IE4 (super premium)

3.2 Variable Frequency Drive (VFD)
A VFD controls motor speed by adjusting the frequency and voltage of the power supply.
Benefits:
- Energy savings of 30–50% on fans and pumps
- Soft start eliminates mechanical stress
- Precise speed control

VFD Parameters:
- Input voltage: 380–480V three-phase
- Output frequency range: 0–400 Hz
- Protection: IP20 (indoor), IP54 (outdoor/dusty environments)

3.3 Electrical Panel Safety
- Always de-energize before working on panels (Lockout/Tagout procedure)
- Use insulated tools rated for the working voltage
- Minimum clearance in front of panels: 900mm
- Arc flash boundary must be marked and PPE worn

SECTION 4: PNEUMATIC SYSTEMS
------------------------------
4.1 Overview
Pneumatic systems use compressed air to transmit power. Simpler and cleaner than hydraulics but limited to lower force applications.

4.2 System Components
- Air Compressor: Generates compressed air. Types: reciprocating, rotary screw, centrifugal.
- Air Receiver Tank: Stores compressed air; provides surge capacity.
- FRL Unit (Filter-Regulator-Lubricator): Conditions the air before use.
- Pneumatic Actuators: Cylinders and rotary actuators for motion.
- Solenoid Valves: Electrically controlled directional valves.

4.3 Operating Specifications
- Working pressure: 4–8 bar (typical industrial applications)
- Air quality standard: ISO 8573-1 (Class 1: particles, moisture, oil)
- Recommended pipe size: Based on flow rate and pressure drop calculations
- Max velocity in pipes: 15 m/s to minimize pressure drop and noise

4.4 Maintenance
- Drain condensate from receiver tank daily
- Clean or replace air filter element every 3 months
- Check compressor oil level weekly (oil-lubricated compressors)
- Leak detection survey quarterly (leaks can waste 20-30% of compressed air)

SECTION 5: CONVEYOR SYSTEMS
----------------------------
5.1 Types of Conveyors
- Belt conveyor: Most common, suitable for bulk materials and packaged goods
- Roller conveyor: For boxes and pallets; gravity or motorized
- Screw conveyor: For granular or semi-solid materials (grain, cement, sludge)
- Chain conveyor: Heavy-duty applications, high-temperature environments

5.2 Belt Conveyor Specifications
- Belt speed: 0.5 – 3.5 m/s for most industrial applications
- Maximum inclination: 20° for smooth belts; 30° for cleated belts
- Belt tension: Must be checked and adjusted to prevent slippage
- Load capacity: Calculated based on belt width, speed, and material density

5.3 Safety Features
- Emergency stop pull-cord along entire length
- Belt misalignment switches (stop conveyor if belt drifts)
- Speed monitoring relay (detect belt slip or jam)
- Guard all nip points, pulleys, and drive mechanisms

SECTION 6: INSTRUMENTATION & CONTROL
--------------------------------------
6.1 Common Sensors
- Temperature: Thermocouple (Type K: -200°C to +1260°C), RTD (PT100: -200°C to +850°C)
- Pressure: Bourdon tube gauges, piezoelectric transducers (0–1000 bar)
- Flow: Electromagnetic (conductive liquids), ultrasonic (non-intrusive), Coriolis (mass flow)
- Level: Float switches, ultrasonic level sensors, radar level transmitters

6.2 PLC (Programmable Logic Controller)
A PLC is an industrial computer used to automate processes.
- Scan time: Typically 1–100ms
- Programming languages (IEC 61131-3): Ladder Logic, Function Block Diagram, Structured Text
- I/O types: Digital (24V DC), Analog (4–20mA, 0–10V)
- Safety PLCs: SIL 2/3 rated for safety-critical applications

6.3 SCADA Systems
SCADA (Supervisory Control and Data Acquisition) provides remote monitoring and control.
- Historian: Logs process data for trend analysis
- HMI (Human-Machine Interface): Operator touchscreen displays
- Alarm management: Priority levels 1 (emergency) to 4 (information)

SECTION 7: SAFETY STANDARDS & REGULATIONS
------------------------------------------
7.1 Key Standards
- ISO 45001: Occupational Health & Safety Management
- IEC 61508: Functional Safety of Electrical/Electronic Systems
- OSHA 1910.147: Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) procedures
- NFPA 70E: Electrical safety in the workplace
- EN ISO 13849: Safety of machinery — control systems

7.2 Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)
- Hard hat: Required in all areas with overhead hazards
- Safety glasses: Required at all times in production areas
- Steel-toed boots: Mandatory on all shop floors
- Hearing protection: Required where noise exceeds 85 dB(A)
- Arc flash suit: Required for electrical work above 50V

7.3 Hazardous Area Classification
- Zone 0: Explosive gas atmosphere present continuously
- Zone 1: Explosive atmosphere likely in normal operation
- Zone 2: Explosive atmosphere unlikely but possible
- Equipment must be ATEX certified for Zones 0, 1, and 2
